Sofia Brandt: Thanks for making time today, Rosalind. I'd like to understand how your team uses data on the operations side, especially for grid assets. Where do you want to start?

Rosalind Thorne: Sure. Right now we track transformer and substation health in a few separate systems. Maintenance records are in one place, sensor readings in another, and outage history in a third. My team spends a lot of time pulling them together by hand.

Sofia Brandt: That's common with utilities. When you pull them together, what are you trying to answer?

Rosalind Thorne: Mostly which assets are likely to fail next and where we should send crews first. Leadership also wants a clearer view of how maintenance spending connects to outage reduction.

Sofia Brandt: How is that handled today? Is it spreadsheets, or do you have a reporting layer on top?

Rosalind Thorne: Mostly spreadsheets and a few dashboards one of my analysts built. They work, but they break when a source system changes. Only a couple of people really understand how they're put together.

Sofia Brandt: That's a risk if one of them is out. Who would use a new tool day to day: your analysts, or also the field operations managers?

Rosalind Thorne: Analysts first. The operations managers would mainly want to view the results, so we'd need something simple for them to open and read without help.

Sofia Brandt: That helps. Larkspur is licensed by seat, so knowing who builds and who only views will shape how we scope it. Is there a data quality issue we should know about early?

Rosalind Thorne: Yes. Older sensor data has gaps, and asset naming isn't consistent between systems. I'd want to be honest about that up front rather than discover it halfway through.

Sofia Brandt: Agreed, and it's better to raise it now. My suggestion is that we schedule a working session where you share a sample of the maintenance and sensor data, and we look at how it would join together.

Rosalind Thorne: That works. I'll pick a representative substation and send over a small extract, with names removed where needed.

Sofia Brandt: Perfect. I'll send a short list of the fields that would be most useful, plus a few times for the working session.
